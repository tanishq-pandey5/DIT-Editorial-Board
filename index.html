About Pratibimb

Pratibimb (प्रतिबिंब — meaning "Reflection") is the flagship annual print and digital journal of DIT University. Established in 1998, the publication serves as an enduring chronicle of student voice, cutting-edge engineering research, creative arts, and campus milestones across 135 masterfully designed pages.

This digital 3D edition pairs high-resolution imagery with natural paper curl physics and an interactive brass magnifying loupe, bringing the tactile warmth of the physical magazine into your web browser.
